# Game audio

The editable catalogue is **Assets/Resources/GameAudioCatalog.asset**. Its 72 entries reference the supplied files in **Assets/Sounds**; each entry has an independent volume. The existing master-volume slider controls every source through AudioListener.volume. Music and long ambience/vehicle loops stream in the background. The original audio files are preserved.

- MENU loops **main theme moon2**; credits play **happysong**, fading between scene states.
- Radio stations are **radio11**, **radio2**, **radio3**, **main theme moon2**, **happysong**. The radio remains an actual 3D emitter, including when carried/thrown; old duplicate radio11 references do not add a sixth station. Existing extra custom stations remain supported. **Radio Volume** is 0.3, independent of menu/credits volume. **Radio Track Gains** match the tracks to the quietest track's gated RMS, without boosting or changing the audio files; their order follows the station list above.
- Interior/exterior ambience blends as the active camera enters/leaves the base. **Untitled** is a very quiet 3D ventilation source named **Base / quiet spatial ventilation**. Move that runtime anchor to preview another position.
- Lunar dirt, concrete and metal riding loops crossfade by the surface under the rover. Mixed LAND triangles resolve their actual submesh material even when the imported mesh is not readable; the bridge is metal.
- Rover engine, acceleration and surface loops follow speed; engine/rolling pitch changes with speed, rolling is suppressed in flight. **engineworking** is enabled; the unpleasant **slowdown** braking cue is disabled. Low battery repeats at most every 18 seconds. Radiation, collection and meteor impact have their respective cues.
- Network sounds share one channel: connecting uses a 0.32s network cue, exiting uses 0.55s, both with a short fade. Each transition to **NO UPLINK**, including selecting a different unavailable satellite, plays up to 0.6s of **losing_connection** with no cooldown between transitions. A battery shutdown plays up to 2s instead, even if the uplink was already unavailable. Remaining in the same failure state does not loop the cue; a new warning replaces the previous one rather than layering it. Connecting/receiver retuning and the intercepted-carrier screen do not trigger this warning. Returning to the PC immediately stops network cues and hacker laughter. **hacker_laugh** starts on the incoming attack event, including attacks through the base relay.
- While the rover feed is active, managed world sounds and registered pistol sources receive low/high-pass and distortion effects. They grow stronger with link noise and fade out when returning to the human camera. No extra listener is created.
- A playing radio remains audible from the disabled **operator camera's** room position while driving the rover, regardless of rover distance. Its existing source temporarily uses a 2D mix with room-distance attenuation, room-relative stereo pan, 55% volume and a fixed 1800 Hz low-pass. This local radio sound does not follow uplink noise/distortion. Returning to the human camera restores normal 3D playback and volume without restarting or duplicating the track; station changes and the master volume still apply.
- Tool pickup/drop, pistol pickup/drop/fire/reload, apple destruction/eating, suspicious apples, doors, physical buttons, crane collection, pipe exit and final robot tests use their mapped cues. Existing custom pistol clips take precedence; the catalogue supplies fallback shot/full reload sounds.
- Picking up radio, ordinary apples and suspicious apples uses **tool_pickup**. Closing an open toolbox repeats **toolbox_open**; an already closed toolbox stays silent. A successful rover cargo unload plays **drop_inside_pipe** at the rover, as well as the existing sound when each part leaves the pipe.
- Both ordinary pistol shots (including custom shot clips) and Crazy Cosmonauts shots fade over their final 0.16s, shortened for very short clips. Each shot has its own voice and fade, so successive shots keep their attacks. **lamp_destroyed** volume is 0.95.
- **turnscreenon** plays after the **robot_check** clip ends and fades out at its tail. The three skipped crane fragments are not used; **robohand_full** accompanies crane collection.
- Wrench/screwdriver audio follows actual thread motion, fades out when movement stops, and does not layer repeated whole clips. Small brown rust particles come from accepted fastener turns; holding without progress produces neither rust nor work audio.
- Human footsteps alternate step1–step4 without immediate repeats, with at least 0.34s running / 0.5s walking between steps. No footsteps during flight, noclip, menus or PC/rover focus. Landings use dirtbang according to impact speed.
- Crazy Cosmonauts uses its separate shot/reload/step/damage clips. Its world is remote, so game sounds are mixed at the PC listener rather than lost at the distant arena. Zombie death/roar works in the PC game and the world; roars have long randomized gaps and may react to hits.
- **bridge** is tied to lowering, **gate_opening** to gate opening, **dirtbang** to landings, **metalbang** to impacts on metal/cargo/large equipment, **zombang** to zombie contact, **fencebang** to fences/gates. Impact volume follows relative speed; cooldowns prevent one contact from producing a sound every physics tick.
- PC/menu/settings hovers and clicks, keyboard input, Escape, order acceptance, login and purchase/dating feedback use their mapped sounds. Hovering the title letters and selecting a toolbox tool uses hover_big_buttons.
- The rover's **H** tuner uses **pc_icon_select** when entering a channel jack, tuner socket or Take Over Bot button, and when clicking them. Take Over Bot plays **pc_confirm** on successful carrier lock or **error_on_computer** for an empty/jammed tuner. Hover sound occurs on target entry rather than every IMGUI repaint; cancelling a held cable with RMB clicks once. All cues follow the existing master volume and rover-feed distortion.

MoonFinder replies wait 0.6–1.1 seconds, then type at 60 characters/second with short **keyboard.mp3** ticks. Player messages appear immediately. Choices stay unavailable until the incoming reply finishes; this also prevents repeated-choice submissions. Background chats finish silently; changing profiles and English/Russian text remains supported. **Characters Per Second** and **Reply Delay** are editable on MoonFinderApp. Romance rewards are delivered when the reply finishes.

The opening radio briefing plays a short **radio_voice** tick per word, paced to the line's duration. Space still advances one line; Escape skips the briefing.

Apple Station plays **dispencer.mp3** at the start of a successful dispensing/shake cycle. Busy or blocked attempts do not play the mechanism sound. It uses 3D playback and the existing master volume.

The operator flashlight toggles with **F** (rebindable in settings). Both manual switching directions play **Assets/Sounds/New/flashlight.mp3** through the master volume. Its beam follows the human camera and is hidden during PC/rover focus, then resumes its previous state without an extra switching sound. **OperatorFlashlight** exposes range, intensity, cone angle and color.
